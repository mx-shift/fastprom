FastPROM updater

Change the ROM images, firmware and fabric of a FastPROM board over its USB-C port.

This browser cannot talk to USB devices. The updater needs WebUSB, which Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers have on desktop and Android. Open this page in one of them.

To look around without a board, open the simulated board.

3. Firmware and fabric

Update

The board restarts into its update mode, is written and checked, and restarts again. Leave it plugged in until it is done; if the power goes, connect again and run the update again: the board is then in its update mode or on its old firmware, never on half of the new.

4. Recovery

For a board that USB cannot reach - new and blank, or with a damaged update mode - through its serial pads and the chip's own boot loader. It writes everything the board needs: its update mode, firmware, fabric and settings. You need a 3.3 V USB-serial adapter (not 5 V) and a desktop Chrome or Edge.

This browser cannot talk to serial ports. Recovery needs Web Serial, which desktop Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers have (not on phones).

  1. Clip the programming fixture onto J3, the SOICbite pads on both faces of the upper board's edge at its pin 1 end: a SOIC-8 test clip, or a clip with pogo pins, wired to a 3.3 V USB-UART adapter. Pin 1 is on top, at the dot. Cross TX and RX:

    J3, the SOICbite pads (1-4 on top, 8-5 under them)
    PinSignalConnect to
    1+3.3 Vthe fixture's 3.3 V, to power a bare board (150 mA or more)
    2BOOT03.3 V in the fixture, to select the loader
    4GNDthe adapter's GND
    5UART0 RXthe adapter's TX
    6UART0 TXthe adapter's RX
    8NRSTpull to GND to reset

  2. Power the board (its USB-C, the host socket, or 3.3 V on pin 1) and reset it with the fixture on and BOOT0 held high: pull pin 8 to GND, or power it off and on. The board now waits for the page, its socket off.
