# FastPROM

## Schematics

The two stack sheets follow `docs/13-schematic-conventions.md`: sheet size
and flow, captioned blocks, power in its own block, connectors in physical
pin order with labels only at their pins, one symbol per part, no buses,
and tables instead of explanations. Read it before changing
`tools/gen_wired.py`, `tools/schlib.py` or `tools/grade_schematic.py`, and
keep it current when a convention changes.
